Sales Cloud overview

Start with the data model, then move to process flow, pricing, and forecasting.

Sales Cloud Data Model

The core Sales Cloud objects model the revenue lifecycle. Understanding these relationships is essential for any Salesforce developer.

Core Objects

  • Lead — prospect before qualification.
  • Account — company or partner record.
  • Contact — person linked to an account.
  • Opportunity — potential revenue deal.
  • Product — catalog item sold through Opportunity.

Relationships

ObjectRelationship
ContactLookup to Account
OpportunityLookup to Account
OpportunityLineItemMaster-detail to Opportunity, lookup to Product
QuoteLookup to Opportunity
PricebookEntryLookup to Price Book and Product

Sales Cloud Rules

  • Leads are converted into Account/Contact/Opportunity.
  • Opportunities use Stage and Probability to track progress.
  • Products are attached to Opportunities via OpportunityLineItems.
  • Price Books manage standard and custom pricing.

Lead Conversion Flow

Lead conversion is the gateway from marketing to sales. Know which fields carry over and what can be automatically created.

Convert Lead

  • Create Account and Contact from Lead.
  • Create Opportunity optionally.
  • Map Lead fields to Account/Contact/Opportunity fields.
  • Retain history via Lead History and Activities.

Developer Notes

  • Use Database.LeadConvert for automation.
  • Custom mapping happens in Lead Field Mapping.
  • Ensure required Account/Contact fields are populated.
  • Converted leads become read-only.

Common Interview Points

  • Explain when to convert a lead manually vs automatically.
  • Mention how lead assignment rules and queues work first.
  • Note that campaign influence stays on converted account.

Product, Price Book, Quote

Sales Cloud pricing objects determine how products and discounts appear on opportunities and quotes.

Products & Price Books

  • Product defines the item or service.
  • Price Book groups prices; standard price book is required.
  • PricebookEntry links a product to a price book.
  • Opportunities use price book entries to create line items.

Quote Process

  • Create a quote from an opportunity.
  • Sync quote line items with opportunity line items.
  • Generate PDF quote documents.
  • Use quote templates for consistent formatting.

Salesforce Rules

  • Opportunity line items must use the opportunity's price book.
  • Products can be active/inactive; only active products are sellable.
  • Quote sync overwrites opportunity data when synced.

Forecasting and Sales Operations

Forecasting turns opportunity data into predictable revenue. Understand the difference between collaborative and custom forecasts.

Forecast Basics

  • Forecast categories map to opportunity stages.
  • Commit types: commit, best case, pipeline.
  • Forecasts can be by owner, product family, or territory.
  • Forecast adjustments allow manual overrides.

Sales Processes

  • Sales process defines which opportunity stages are available.
  • Record types can use different page layouts per process.
  • Validation rules enforce stage-specific requirements.

Developer checklist

  • Use Apex or Flow to update quotes when opportunity changes.
  • Protect forecast fields with field level security.
  • Use custom objects only when standard product/opportunity model isn't sufficient.
